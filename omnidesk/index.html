Backup & privacy

Local-first workspace

Your tasks, emails and application tracking details are kept in this browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to our server, and your phone and laptop do not automatically sync. Avoid entering passwords, passport numbers, bank information, or other sensitive documents.

Free does not mean unlimited automation.

This version works without API billing or an AI subscription. Automated publishing, email inbox reading, university portal submissions and real-time message monitoring require additional official permissions, a backend and possibly paid API usage.
